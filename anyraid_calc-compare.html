How this is calculated: each disk is split into tiles of the chosen tile size (rounded down). In each round, the stripe width disks with the most free tiles are picked and one tile from each is assigned to a new stripe, until there are no longer enough disks with free capacity. AVAIL additionally accounts for the spa_slop_shift reserve (default 5 = 1/32 ≈ 3.125%).
